            Ollama address

            Where Ollama is running. Usually http://127.0.0.1:11434; point it at another machine on your home network to run inference there.

            Watched folder

            Documents dropped here are picked up automatically. Point this at a NAS or cloud-synced folder if you want the family archive to live somewhere durable.

            Document OCR

            Currently: built-in engine. The built-in engine (Tesseract) needs no setup. For higher-quality OCR of scans and photos, pick a local Ollama vision model such as glm-ocr:latest (pull it first with ollama pull glm-ocr:latest). Nothing leaves your machine either way. Best on a machine with a GPU: without one a page can take minutes, and an upload that runs over budget falls back to the built-in engine.

  ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
  User-Generated Content / does the app include messaging or user-to-user
  interaction? .................................... YES  (see note)
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The app has family messaging (1:1 and group channels) and a shared note
  board. This is a CLOSED group: every participant is an account on one
  household's self-hosted server, created by that household's admin. There is
  no public feed, no discovery of strangers, no way for an outside user to
  reach a household's server or content.

  Apple may still expect UGC safeguards (Guideline 1.2): a way to report
  content, block a user, and a published point of contact. TODAY THE APP HAS
  NONE OF THESE. Before a public submission, either:
    (a) add in-app report/block + a moderation contact, or
    (b) ship messaging as a later update and submit 1.0 without the Messages
        tab, or
    (c) go the TestFlight-internal route (../RELEASE.md), where this is not
        evaluated.
  This is the single most likely public-review rejection. Flagged in
  SUBMISSION.md → "Known blockers".

Expected resulting rating: 4+ (with the UGC caveat above resolved).

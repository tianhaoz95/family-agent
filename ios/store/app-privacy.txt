APP PRIVACY QUESTIONNAIRE
(App Store Connect → your app → App Privacy → "Get Started" / Edit)

This is app-level, not per-version, and it is cross-checked against
FamilyAgent/PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy. That manifest declares: NSPrivacyTracking =
false, no NSPrivacyCollectedDataTypes, and one required-reason API
(UserDefaults, reason CA92.1). Keep the answers below in step with it.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q: Do you or your third-party partners collect data from this app?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    NO — select "No, we do not collect data from this app".

Rationale (keep for your records):
  - Everything the user types, uploads, photographs or dictates is sent to a
    server the USER runs on their own machine. The developer has no access to
    that server and no copy of anything on it.
  - There is no analytics SDK, no crash reporter, no advertising identifier, no
    third-party network call of any kind from the app.
  - The app stores on-device only: server URL, display names, a couple of view
    preferences (UserDefaults), and a session token (Keychain). None of it
    leaves the device except to the user's own server.
  - "Data collected" in Apple's sense means data transmitted off the device AND
    linked to the user/device by you or a partner. Neither happens here.

If Apple's flow forces you past the first question, the honest position is still
"Data Not Collected" for every category. Do not declare Contacts, Messages,
Photos, Audio, etc. just because the app can access them — access used solely to
deliver an obvious in-app feature, sent only to the user's own server, is not
collection.

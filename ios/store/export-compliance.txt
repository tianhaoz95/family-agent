(Asked at build submission, or per-build under TestFlight / the version)

Info.plist already sets:  ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = false

So App Store Connect will NOT prompt you. If it ever does, the answers are:

  Does your app use encryption? ................................. YES
    (It makes HTTPS connections and uses the system Keychain.)

  Does your app qualify for any of the exemptions? ............. YES
    Exemption: the app uses only:
      - encryption built into the operating system (TLS via URLSession,
        Keychain), and
      - standard encryption for authentication (the bearer-token flow over
        TLS to the user's own server).
    It implements no proprietary or non-standard cryptography of its own.
    (The password-vault cryptography lives entirely in the SERVER, agent-core;
    the iOS app never performs it.)

  => "Non-exempt encryption": NO. No CCATS / year-end self-classification
     report is required. This matches ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = false.

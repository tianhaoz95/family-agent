Version 1.1.2

- The menu drawer is easier to use: tapping an item anywhere along the row now
  registers, instead of only on the icon or label.
- You can now dismiss the keyboard in Chat and Messages — swipe down over the
  conversation, or tap the new hide-keyboard button above the keys.

Earlier in 1.1.x:
- Pairing over Tailscale: scan the desktop's QR code (Settings → Pair a phone)
  to connect whether you're on home Wi-Fi, cellular, or another network.
- The desktop "Pair a phone" list shows every way to reach the server and you
  pick the one your phone can use.
- Fixed invisible text in the manual address field on devices set to Dark Mode.
- More reliable automatic discovery on the home network.

Requires the free Family Agent desktop app on a Mac at home. Feedback and issues:
https://github.com/tianhaoz95/family-agent

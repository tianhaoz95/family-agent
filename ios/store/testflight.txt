WHAT TO TEST  (per build — the one field internal testing requires)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  First build. Please check:
   - The app finds your Family Agent server on the same Wi-Fi, or accepts the
     address from the desktop app's Settings when typed in.
   - Sign in with your household account.
   - Chat: ask something that needs your data ("what's on this week?"). Replies
     take 30-120s on local hardware — that's expected.
   - Events: add an event with +, check the week and month views.
   - Documents: scan or attach a photo of a bill; confirm it's filed with dates.
   - Messages: DM another family member; try "@agent ..." in a channel.
   - Board, Routines, Vault: open each, add an item.
   - Voice: tap the mic to dictate; hold it to talk and hear the reply.
  Report anything that hangs, crashes, or looks wrong on your device size.
